Advice answers

Why advice goes through tools next

"How can I save 1,000 AED a month?"

Three cuts that add up to about 295 AED, then "these adjustments should help you reach your goal".

"Give me 5 best places to cut"

Three places, priced in dollars ($484, $1230, $176) when the data is in AED.

"Where can I save next month?"

Describes government fees as "parking fines or utility bills", which belong to other categories.

Every number the model was given was real; its arithmetic and reasoning were not. The locked tab shows the proposed fix running: fixed analysis tools compute every figure, and the text only ranks and phrases them.
