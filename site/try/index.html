Ask your bank statement anything.

Drop in your bank SMS export (ADCB, Liv, Mashreq, FAB or ADIB) and Ledger runs the same pipeline as the research notebooks: it throws out OTPs and notices, parses each message, cleans the merchant names, sorts every expense into a category with the trained model, and flags the ones it isn't sure about. Then it shows where your money goes and answers questions in plain English (or Arabic). The numbers always come from SQL run on your data, never from a language model's guess.

🔒 Your file never leaves this tab

  • Everything runs in your browser: SMS parsing, categorising, the SQL database and the optional AI model.
  • No server, no uploads, no cookies, no storage. Close the tab and it's gone.
  • The page's security policy only lets it download code and model weights. There is nowhere for it to send your data.
How can I check?

Open your browser's developer tools → Network tab, then load your file. You'll see no requests carrying your data; only library and (if you choose) model downloads. You can also load the page, switch off Wi-Fi, and then use it: everything except the AI model keeps working.

Drop your bank SMS export here

An Excel or CSV file with Date and Message columns, or an SMS Backup & Restore .xml file. Any other transaction spreadsheet (bank statement, Actual Budget export) works too.

Bank SMS go through the full pipeline: OTP and notice filtering, bank-specific parsing, merchant cleaning, currency conversion, duplicate removal and the calibrated categoriser. Transaction spreadsheets need a date, a description and an amount (or debit / credit) column. The sample is synthetic: a made-up Dubai household, 12 months, no real person's data.
